# Code of Conduct

Misgeret welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for different levels of technical and financial knowledge.
- Protect privacy. Never request or share real credentials, transactions, database files, exports, or identifying screenshots.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful behavior directly.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer. For security vulnerabilities, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
